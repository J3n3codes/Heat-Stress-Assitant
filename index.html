About this project

The Heat Stress Assistant is a retrieval-based chatbot prototype. It matches user questions with predefined training examples and retrieves an associated response from a response template database.

The project explores lightweight question matching and evidence-informed information retrieval.

Important Safety Notice

This chatbot is an educational prototype and does not provide medical diagnosis or individualized treatment. Confusion, fainting, collapse, seizures, or altered consciousness may indicate a medical emergency. Stop activity and seek urgent medical help immediately.
