Recovery reminders

💧 Restoring hydration

Replace fluids lost through sweating. Electrolytes may be useful when sweat losses are substantial.

🌡️ Cooling after heat exposure

Rest in a cool environment and consider appropriate cooling strategies.

🥗 Nutrition and recovery

Balanced meals, carbohydrates, fluids and adequate nutrition support recovery.

⚠️ Warning symptoms

Confusion, fainting, collapse, seizures or altered consciousness may indicate a medical emergency. Stop activity and seek urgent medical help immediately.
